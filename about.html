What is CUIMS?

CUIMS (Chandigarh University Information Management System) is an integrated portal designed to provide seamless access to student services, learning management system, staff login, and admission processes.

Our mission is to make education and administration more accessible, efficient, and transparent for students and staff alike. Through CUIMS, users can manage academic records, access courses, and stay updated with the latest university announcements.
